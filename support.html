Support & Help

✉️Contact us

Email us and we will get back to you. You do not need a Turnstile account to reach us.

hq.turnstile@gmail.com

We aim to answer general questions within 2 business days. Reports of abuse are reviewed within 24 hours.

💬Get help from inside the app

The fastest way to reach us is the built-in support form, which attaches your app version and device details so we can debug without a back-and-forth:

Settings → Support → Contact Support

🚩Report someone

If another member's name, photo or behaviour is inappropriate, you can report them from their profile or from your Friends list:

Open their profile → ⋮ → Report User

Pick a reason, add any detail that helps, and choose whether to block them at the same time. Reports are confidential — the person you report is never told who reported them. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours, and we remove content or accounts that break our Terms & Conditions.

🛡️Block someone

Blocking removes someone from your friends, hides you from their search results, and stops them sending you friend requests, nudges or kudos:

Open their profile → ⋮ → Block User

You can see everyone you have blocked, and unblock them, at Settings → Privacy & Tracking → Blocked Users.

🗑️Delete your account

You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time:

Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account

This permanently removes your profile, workout history, weekly summaries, friends and uploaded photos. It cannot be undone.
