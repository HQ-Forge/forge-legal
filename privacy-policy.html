Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-03

This Privacy Policy explains how Khalil Garaali ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the Forge mobile application ("Application" or "Forge"). Forge is a fitness-tracking app that lets you set workout goals, check in at gyms and other locations, and connect with friends for accountability.

By creating an account or using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Application.

Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The data controller responsible for your personal information is Khalil Garaali, the developer of Forge. You can reach us at hq.forgeapp@gmail.com regarding any privacy matter.

Information We Collect

Information you provide to us:

  • Account information: your email address, username, and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and (if available) your Google profile photo.
  • Password: if you register with email and password, your password is handled and stored securely by our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication). We do not have access to your plain-text password.
  • Profile photo: if you upload a custom avatar, the image you select is stored on our servers.
  • Saved locations ("Arenas"): the gyms or other locations you choose to save, including their names and geographic coordinates.
  • Workout data: workout start and end times, duration, the arena or location associated with each workout, and your weekly goals.
  • Social data: your friends, friend requests, blocked users, and the nudges and kudos you send and receive.

Information collected automatically:

  • Approximate and precise device location (see "Location Information" below).
  • Device and technical information, such as your device type, operating system, and IP address, collected by our infrastructure providers.
  • Push notification tokens, so we can deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • Basic usage information necessary to operate the Application (for example, timestamps of your activity).

We do not collect your date of birth, age, or gender, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

Location Information

Forge is a location-based fitness app, so location data is central to how it works. With your permission, the Application accesses your device's precise location to:

  • Detect when you are within approximately 200 meters of a saved arena so you can start a verified workout;
  • While the Application is open, periodically check your proximity to saved arenas (approximately once per minute) to prompt you to start a workout;
  • Display your position on maps and show the name of your current city through reverse geocoding.

You control location access through your device's operating system settings and through the in-app Location Tracking toggle. If you disable location access, location-dependent features (such as verified check-ins and proximity alerts) will not work. We do not track your location in the background when the Application is closed.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you;
  • Provide the core features of the Application, including workout tracking, goals, stats, streaks, and location-based check-ins;
  • Enable social features such as friend connections, notifications, nudges, and kudos;
  • Send you notifications you have enabled;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Application and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • Respond to your requests and provide support;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases for processing (for users in the EEA/UK): we process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the Application), on the basis of your consent (for example, location access and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time), to pursue our legitimate interests (such as securing and improving the Application), and to comply with legal obligations.

How Your Information Is Shared

With other users. Forge is a social application. Your username, display name, avatar, workout statistics (such as total workouts and streaks), and recent activity are visible to other users, including your friends and users who find you through search. Please consider this before adding information to your profile. Your exact saved arena locations and your email address are not shown to other users.

With service providers. We share information with trusted third parties that process data on our behalf so we can operate the Application. These providers are only permitted to use your information to provide services to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and Cloud Functions) — our core backend, which stores your account data, workouts, photos, and social data.
  • Google (Google Sign-In, Google Maps, and the Google Places API) — for authentication and location/map features.
  • Expo — application infrastructure and delivery of push notifications.

For legal reasons. We may disclose your information when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to comply with a law, regulation, subpoena, or other legal process; to enforce our Terms & Conditions; to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us; or to investigate fraud or security issues.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter as needed to comply with our legal obligations or resolve disputes.

You may also contact us at hq.forgeapp@gmail.com to request deletion, and we will respond within a reasonable time.
